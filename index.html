About Me

Building Solutions That Matter

Software developer, game creator, and AI enthusiast

I'm Freduah Dominic Kwakye — a software developer, game creator, and AI enthusiast who builds technology that solves real problems.

I've built SABAODY AI, a pirate-themed AI web app with 12+ features including AI chat, image generation, video creation, text-to-speech, weather, lyrics, translation, and more. I created SABAODY Bot, the most advanced anime RPG game on WhatsApp with card wars, live duels, a full economy, 15+ mini-games, and a casino.

I built Duapa, a voice-first AI store builder for Ghanaian micro-sellers. Sellers create online stores by talking to a WhatsApp assistant in local languages like Twi, Ga, and Hausa. It solves a real problem — 80% of Ghanaian micro-sellers have no digital storefront.

I'm also building Duskfall, a 3D zombie survival game in Godot 4.7 with automated APK/EXE builds via GitHub Actions.

As STEM 3 Engineer for my school's robotics team, I developed practical engineering skills and learned to collaborate on complex projects.

My mission: Build technology that empowers people and solves real problems in Africa.
